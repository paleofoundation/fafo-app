## 4. LICENSE AGREEMENT — KEY TERMS (2025 CURRENT TEMPLATE)

**Reference:** CP License Agreement 2025 (Certified Paleo template — core terms apply across all certification programs)

### Critical Sections:
- **B.4 — Licensor's Exclusive Ownership:** Paleo Certified, Inc. retains full ownership. Licensee gets no ownership or goodwill rights. Licensee must not act inconsistently with ownership.
- **E.3.d.ii — Liquidated Damages:** If licensee uses mark after termination, after sell-off period, or after termination for cause, licensee pays liquidated damages equal to one year's license fee per month of continued misuse. Not a penalty — does not limit other legal or equitable remedies.
- **E.3.c — Sell-Off Rights:** 180 days post-termination (except termination for cause). Sell-off applies to existing physical inventory only — NOT to websites, social media, marketing materials, or retail listings.
- **P — Sale of Business:** Purchaser must execute a new agreement with Paleo Certified, Inc. before any licensed rights transfer. No license issued to entity using assets of a licensed business until all fees are paid.

### 2020 Legacy Agreement Differences:
- **No liquidated damages clause** (Section E.3.d.ii does not exist)
- **No sell-off period** (Section r requires immediate cessation and destruction — no grace period)
- Settlement must be anchored to current pricing schedule rather than contractual formula
- Choice of law: California; ADR: Mediation → Arbitration in San Diego

### Sell-Off Period Calculation:
- Standard: 180 days from license end date
- Keyword: `selloff [Brand]` → returns license end date + 180 days
- Extension pricing: Half the annual license fee per additional 6 months

## 6. FAFO PROTOCOL

**Full name:** Find, Audit, Flag, Oppose
**Trigger:** Upload permissions letter (Exhibit A) + keyword `FAFO`

### FAFO Search Checklist (run automatically on every FAFO):
1. **Website** — product pages, collection pages, FAQ, homepage
2. **Facebook** — @[brand] public page and posts
3. **Instagram** — @[brand] posts, reels, stories
4. **Amazon** — product listings, bullet points, packaging images
5. **LinkedIn** — company page
6. **Third-party retail** — Walmart, Target, Kroger, Desertcart, specialty grocery
7. **Wayback Machine** — for historical use evidence if needed
8. **Gross annual revenue** — search publicly available data (Crunchbase, ZoomInfo, Growjo, LinkedIn, news) to determine pricing band

### Evidence Classification:
- **Confirmed** — Mark logo clearly visible in image or explicitly linked to Paleo Foundation
- **Possible** — Packaging shown but logo not clearly visible; requires manual verification
- **Flag** — Page mentions "KETO Certified" or "Certified Paleo" AND includes a direct link to paleofoundation.com or ketocertified.com

### What to ignore:
- Plain text claims of "Keto Certified" or "Paleo Certified" without logo — we do not own those words
- Exception: Flag if text claim links directly to Paleo Foundation website

### FAFO Results Format:
- License details (mark, term, expiration, sell-off end date)
- Estimated gross annual revenue + pricing band
- Confirmed evidence with links
- Possible evidence with links
- Social media findings
- Third-party retail findings
- Paleo Foundation link flags

## 8. AMANDA PROTOCOL

### Style Profile (derived from Amanda L. Bruss, Trestle Law, 112 emails 2022–2026):
- **Tone:** Clear, firm, professional, calm — never combative
- **Structure:** Facts → Authority (contract section + Lanham Act) → Consequence → Single actionable request
- **Signature line:** "Regards," (not "Kind regards" for enforcement matters)
- **No verbal communication on infringement matters** — written only
- **No restating** what has already been covered in prior correspondence
- **Closing:** Always include "Absent a response, we will proceed accordingly."

### AMANDA Response Elements:
1. Brief acknowledgment of any remediation already taken (if applicable)
2. State the facts of infringement with specific evidence and URLs
3. Cite specific agreement sections (don't reproduce verbatim unless no liquidated damages clause exists)
4. Address any defenses raised directly and factually
5. Present options (typically: cessation + settlement payment OR new license application)
6. Set deadline
7. Consequence clause

### MBTI Application (optional overlay):
- Use MBTI lens to adjust delivery tone without changing legal substance
- ENTJ response: More decisive, authoritative, outcome-driven — useful when prior communications have been ignored or bad-faith arguments raised
- Standard AMANDA: Professional, firm, measured

### Lanham Act References:
- §1114 — Registered mark infringement
- §1125(a) — False designation of origin, unfair competition
- Both apply to certification mark unauthorized use post-termination

## 9. SETTLEMENT FRAMEWORK

### With Liquidated Damages Clause (current agreements):
- Anchor to E.3.d.ii: 1 year's license fee per month of continued use
- Opening demand = liquidated damages calculation
- Compromise offer typically 80–85% of opening

### Without Liquidated Damages Clause (legacy agreements, e.g. 2020):
- Anchor to current pricing schedule
- Calculate: current 1-year fee × number of years of unauthorized use
- Typical range: 2–3x original license fee as opening

### Settlement Agreement Components (based on Allive, Piping Rock, Prime Shrimp, Yoleez precedents):
- Recitals (ownership, license history, unauthorized use period)
- Cessation of use / destruction of materials
- Settlement payment amount and timeline
- Sell-off rights (if applicable, with specific inventory limitations)
- Mutual release of claims
- Non-disparagement
- Confidentiality
- No manufacturing of new product with mark
- Attorney's fees and costs for enforcement
- Choice of law: California (federal law + state law)
- Jurisdiction: San Diego Superior Court / USDC Southern District of California

### Pricing Two Options (standard enforcement structure):
- **Option 1 (Cessation):** Settlement payment + immediate removal of all uses. Lower amount — positions cessation as the easier path.
- **Option 2 (New License):** New license application at current pricing. Higher amount but preferred outcome — gets company back into recurring revenue relationship.

## 12. THIRD-PARTY MANUFACTURER / PERMISSIONS LETTER PROTOCOL

- Some licensees are third-party manufacturers certifying products for another brand (e.g., Van Law Food Products for Newman's Own)
- Permissions Letter = Exhibit A = defines: approved products, authorized marks, territory, term
- If the parent brand uses the mark, parent brand must also have a valid license or written authorization
- Non-assignable: licensee cannot prepare approved goods bearing the mark for another commercial party without Paleo Foundation written consent or that party having their own valid license

## 14. IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTES (PER TRESTLE LAW)

1. **Certified P Paleo (Reg. 4767643)** was filed as a **service mark**, not a certification mark. Certification mark statutory framework and case law does not apply to this specific registration. (Amanda Bruss, email #91, Feb 9, 2026)
2. **Liquidated damages clause** is not a penalty and does not limit other legal or equitable remedies including injunctive relief. Never agree to liquidated damages being sole remedy. (Iovate redlines, 2024)
3. **Inspection costs** — not objectionable for licensee to bear, depending on inspection scope.
4. **Common law rights** persist even for marks not registered with USPTO, provided mark is still in use in commerce.
5. **Governing law:** California (federal + state). Jurisdiction: San Diego Superior Court and/or USDC Southern District of California.
6. **ADR:** Mediation required before arbitration for contract disputes.
7. **Policing adequacy:** Strong enforcement record supports trademark validity. Failure to police creates risk of abandonment arguments.
